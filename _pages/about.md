I am an Associate Professor in the Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (IPADS), Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
My current research focuses on **AI/Agentic Operating Systems**: building operating systems for AI agents and exploring how agents can reshape OS design.
My research spans four directions:

- **Agent sandbox:** [DeltaBox]({{ '/publications/#dong2026deltabox' | relative_url }}) and [DeltaBox Lite]({{ '/projects/deltabox-lite/' | relative_url }}).
- **Agent memory:** memory abstractions and management for AI agents.
- **Agentic kernel:** bringing agent capabilities into OS kernels.
- **Generative OS:** generating OS components from specifications, including SpecFS/SYSSPEC (FAST 2026).

My **past work** includes serverless computing (Catalyzer, ServerlessBench, and Molecule), trusted execution environments (Penglai TEE), and hardware/software co-design (the RISC-V XPC extension).

I am also working on open-sourced communities, including RISC-V (SPMP TG chair), openEuler (RISC-V SIG maintainer) and OpenHarmony (RISC-V and TEE SIG maintainer).
